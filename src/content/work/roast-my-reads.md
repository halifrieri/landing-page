## Context

Spotify Wrapped works because it is a little bit mean. Reading stats are usually presented as
gentle encouragement — *you read 34 books, wonderful* — which is far less fun than the truth.
`roast-my-reads` takes a Goodreads year in review and gives it an opinion.
